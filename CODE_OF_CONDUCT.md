# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in FreeCoder MediKit to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent; critique ideas, not people
- Give and accept feedback gracefully
- Credit others' work

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Publishing others' private information
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or malicious code

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainer via a private channel. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions that violate this code.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
